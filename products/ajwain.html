Ajwain

Our export-grade Ajwain (Carom Seeds) represents the crowning achievement of India's summer harvest (June to September), sourced exclusively from the prime growing regions of Gujarat, Maharashtra, and Madhya Pradesh. As one of the world's preeminent ajwain producers, India provides the ideal terroir for developing these exceptional Trachyspermum ammi seeds. Treasured for centuries in Ayurvedic traditions, our Ajwain delivers unmatched therapeutic benefits—revolutionizing digestive health, providing superior respiratory relief, and bolstering immune function through its natural antimicrobial properties. With an impressive essential oil concentration of 2-4%, our premium Ajwain exceeds industry standards and delivers exceptional versatility across culinary applications, pharmaceutical formulations, and therapeutic aromatherapy.
