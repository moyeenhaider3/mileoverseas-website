India's Sesame Seed Industry

India is one of the world's top producers and exporters of sesame seeds (Sesamum indicum), with over 3,000 years of cultivation history. India produces approximately 800,000 tonnes of sesame seeds annually, ranking among the top three global producers alongside Sudan and Myanmar.

Indian Sesame Seeds at a Glance:
  • Scientific Name: Sesamum indicum L.
  • Major Growing States: Gujarat, Rajasthan, West Bengal, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra
  • Harvest Season: June to September (Kharif crop), January to March (Rabi crop)
  • HS Code: 1207.40
  • Oil Content: 48 – 55% (premium grade)
  • Annual Export Value: Over USD 600 million
  • Top Export Markets: China, Vietnam, South Korea, Japan, Turkey, USA, EU

Indian sesame seeds are prized for their exceptional oil content (50–55% in premium Gujarat varieties), nutty flavor profile, and versatile applications across food, cosmetics, and pharmaceutical industries. The unique semi-arid growing conditions of Gujarat and Rajasthan produce sesame with superior quality characteristics compared to African origins.

Sesame Seed Varieties for Export

India exports three primary types of sesame seeds, each serving different market needs:

1. White (Natural) Sesame Seeds

White sesame is the most exported variety from India, accounting for over 70% of total sesame exports. These seeds have a creamy white to off-white color and are valued for their high oil content and mild, nutty flavor.

Parameter Specification

Oil Content 48 – 55%
Moisture <5%
Purity 99% – 99.95%
FFA (Free Fatty Acid) <2%

2. Black Sesame Seeds

Black sesame seeds are a premium variety with higher antioxidant levels and a more intense, earthy flavor. They are particularly popular in East Asian cuisine and traditional medicine.

Parameter Specification
Color Deep black (jet black preferred)
Oil Content 46 – 52%
Moisture <5%
Purity 99% – 99.95%
Size 2.2 – 3.0 mm
Best For Asian cuisine, health foods, black sesame oil, traditional medicine
Key Markets Japan, South Korea, China, Taiwan, USA

3. Hulled Sesame Seeds

Hulled sesame seeds are white sesame with the outer husk (hull) removed through a mechanical or chemical hulling process. This produces a smoother, lighter seed with improved taste and texture — ideal for food-grade applications.

Parameter Specification
Color Pure white (ivory to snow white)
Oil Content 50 – 57%
Moisture <5%
Purity 99.90% – 99.99%
FFA <1.5%
Unhulled Ratio <0.1% (premium grade)
Best For Tahini, hummus, bakery toppings, burger buns, confectionery, sushi garnish
Key Markets EU, USA, Middle East, Japan, Australia

4. Brown Sesame Seeds

Brown sesame is primarily used for oil extraction in Indian domestic and export markets. It has a robust, earthy flavor and is less refined than white varieties.

Parameter Specification
Color Golden brown to reddish brown
Oil Content 45 – 50%
Moisture <6%
Best For Oil extraction, animal feed, industrial applications
Key Markets China, Vietnam, West Africa
Buyer's Tip: For food-grade applications (bakery, tahini, confectionery), always specify hulled sesame seeds with 99.95%+ purity and <1% FFA. For oil extraction, natural white sesame with high oil content (52%+) gives the best yield.

Growing Regions & Harvest Calendar

Major Growing Regions

  • Gujarat (Banaskantha, Sabarkantha, Kutch): India's largest sesame producing state (30%+ of national output). Known for premium white sesame with the highest oil content. Semi-arid climate and sandy loam soils create ideal growing conditions.
  • Rajasthan (Pali, Jalore, Nagaur, Jodhpur): Second largest producer. Desert climate produces robust seeds with good oil content. Both white and brown varieties.
  • West Bengal (Murshidabad, Birbhum): Primary source of black sesame seeds. Two growing seasons (Kharif and Rabi) enable year-round supply.
  • Madhya Pradesh (Tikamgarh, Chhatarpur): Growing production region with increasing organic cultivation.
  • Uttar Pradesh & Maharashtra: Smaller production volumes focused on both white and mixed varieties.

Applications & End Uses

Food Industry

  • Tahini / Sesame Paste: Hulled white sesame — primary ingredient for tahini (Middle East), halva, and hummus
  • Bakery: Hulled and natural sesame for burger buns, bread toppings, breadsticks, and crackers
  • Confectionery: Sesame brittle (chikki/halwa), energy bars, snack mixes, and candy coatings
  • Cooking Oil: Cold-pressed and refined sesame oil — premium cooking oil in Asian cuisines
  • Asian Cuisine: Black and white sesame for sushi, stir-fry, ramen, and rice dishes

Industrial & Non-Food

  • Cosmetics: Sesame oil in skincare, massage oils, and hair care products (rich in vitamin E and sesamol)
  • Pharmaceutical: Carrier oil for Ayurvedic and traditional medicines, supplements
  • Animal Feed: Sesame meal (de-oiled cake) as high-protein animal feed ingredient

Nutritional & Health Benefits

Indian sesame seeds are a nutritional powerhouse, making them increasingly popular in health-conscious markets:

  • High in Healthy Fats: 48–55% oil content with excellent omega-6 to omega-3 ratio
  • Rich in Protein: 17–25% protein content — valuable plant protein source
  • Calcium Rich: Unhulled sesame provides up to 975mg calcium per 100g — more than milk
  • Antioxidants: Contains sesamin and sesamolin — unique lignans with antioxidant properties
  • Iron & Zinc: Excellent source of iron (14.6mg/100g) and zinc (7.8mg/100g)
  • Vitamin E: Natural source of tocopherols for skin health and immune function

These health credentials drive growing demand for sesame seeds in health food, superfood, and clean-label product categories worldwide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of sesame seeds does India export?

India exports White (Natural) Sesame Seeds (48–55% oil, most exported), Black Sesame Seeds (premium, high antioxidants, popular in East Asia), Hulled Sesame Seeds (husk removed, 99.95% purity, for tahini/bakery), and Brown Sesame Seeds (for oil extraction).

What is the HS code for sesame seeds?

The HS code is 1207.40. Specifically, 1207.4010 for seed quality and 1207.4090 for other sesame (food/oil use). Sesame oil is classified under 1515.50.

Which Indian states produce the best sesame?

Gujarat (Banaskantha, Sabarkantha, Kutch) is the largest producer with the highest oil content white sesame. Rajasthan is the second largest producer. West Bengal is the primary source for black sesame seeds.

What is the oil content of Indian sesame seeds?

Indian sesame typically contains 48–55% oil, with premium Gujarat white sesame reaching 50–55%. This is among the highest globally, making Indian sesame excellent for oil extraction. Higher oil content = better quality = premium pricing.
